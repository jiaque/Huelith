resolution and transparency are retained: 4608×3456, 3999×3999 and 1254×1254.

Bow generation prompt:

> Create a production photo-retouching decal asset: ONE elegant burgundy satin hair
> bow, front view, compact symmetrical loops and two short tapered ribbon tails.
> Realistic woven satin texture and subtle dimensional folds, soft neutral diffuse
> studio light, no hard cast shadow. Entire bow fully visible, centered, fills 85
> percent of a square canvas, at least 2048 pixel quality if possible. Genuine
> transparent alpha background, no person, hair, packaging, jewelry, text, watermark,
> border or white backdrop. This is a standalone high resolution PNG hair accessory
> intended to overlay on portrait photographs, not a vector icon or emoji.

The returned image was 1254×1254; it is not labeled as 2048×2048.
